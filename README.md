## Featured project: Ted, your personal gym coach

Ted is an AI strength coach for gym beginners. Every morning it reads your sleep, recovery, training history, and calendar, plans one workout that fits your day, and coaches you through it in a warm voice that never judges. You approve every plan and every weight increase, and if you report pain, Ted stops coaching and points you to a professional.

- [Read the case study](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/ted/): the problem, how Ted works, how I tested it, and how I'd launch it
- [Try the prototype](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/ted/prototype.html): runs in your browser with sample data. Tap "Play Maya's six weeks" for the guided story.

I built Ted as my capstone for Maven's AI Product Management Certification.

## Featured project: SWAN, autonomous exception management

SWAN is a prototype of an autonomous, governed agent for supply chains. It watches connected systems around the clock, investigates disruptions, simulates every option before it acts, and brings a person in only when a decision is theirs to make. All data is fictional.

- [See the project](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/swan/)
- Three demos that play themselves: [incident response](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/swan/incident-response.html), [exception management](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/swan/exceptions.html), and [control tower](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/swan/tower.html)
