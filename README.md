## Featured project: Ted, your personal gym coach

Ted is an AI strength coach for gym beginners. Every morning it reads your sleep, recovery, training history, and calendar, plans one workout that fits your day, and coaches you through it in a warm voice that never judges. You approve every plan and every weight increase, and if you report pain, Ted stops coaching and points you to a professional.

- [Read the case study](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/ted/): the problem, how Ted works, how I tested it, and how I'd launch it
- [Try the prototype](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/ted/prototype.html): runs in your browser with sample data. Tap "Play Maya's six weeks" for the guided story.

I built Ted as my capstone for Maven's AI Product Management Certification.

## Featured project: SWAN, autonomous supply chain manager

SWAN is a prototype of a proactive, always-on agent for supply chains. It watches connected systems around the clock, investigates disruptions, simulates every option before it acts, and brings a person in only when a decision is theirs to make. All data is fictional.

- [Read the case study](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/swan/)
- Three demos that play themselves: [incident response](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/swan/incident-response.html), [exception management](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/swan/exceptions.html), and [control tower](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/swan/tower.html)

## Featured project: AI PM Coach

AI PM Coach is two AI tools for product managers, built to give feedback on product thinking in minutes.

- **Product thinking practice:** talk through a product problem out loud, AI personas or a panel of two or three ask follow-ups, and an AI judge scores your answer on five PM competencies and tells you exactly what to fix.
- **Pitch Panel:** pitch a feature to AI personas of your engineering, architecture, security, program, QA, and UX partners. They co-develop it with you or review it like a real panel, a feature canvas fills in as you talk, and you leave with each panelist's stance and a co-developed feature brief.

- [Read the case study](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/ai-pm-coach/)
- [Try product thinking practice](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/ai-pm-coach/prototype.html) and [Try Pitch Panel](https://himanshia30.github.io/projects/ai-pm-coach/pitch-panel.html): both work best in Chrome or Edge. Add an AI key in Settings (Groq's free tier works) for the AI personas and scoring.
